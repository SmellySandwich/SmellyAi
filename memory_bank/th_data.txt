 -  **__[AR-نصائح للمبتدئين الجزء الأول](https://discord.com/channels/1098493491216121916/1274209111965438104/1274209111965438104)__**

  **Tip1**
In the early adventure stages it is important to have a healer leveled up to keep up with your team! It is recommended is to start with **Pharmacist**   and then swap to **Minister**  when you obtain her. 

 **Tip2** 
**Ranger** :Ranger: is a great hero in adventure mode early game because his 3rd skill when unlocked allows him to auto attack while moving but once you obtain legendary heroes don't hesitate to swap him out. 

 **Tip3** 
Early game do not worry much about your Main team (Queue1) composition especially when you have only 6 or less legendary heroes, it is recommended to just use all of them + Minister or Shaman as a healer. 

 **Tip4**
The ability to level up Herors in Top Heroes is tied to the **Castle Level** and the **Food cost** :Meat: (we will discuss later), For Castle level always check requirements for it and try to prioritize it, unlike other games you do not have to level up everything equally. 

 **Tip5** 
After prioritizing your Castle level, always check your Main Queue heroes and level them up too. 

 **Tip6**
Join an active guild, I can't stress this enough! You miss a lot by not doing so! 

 **Tip7** 
Keep up with Open world Bounties :Bounty_Mission:  try to not skip them, these Bounties get better the more you use them! Making them really valuable source
of gain in end game. 

 **Tip8**
Open world Rallies are great stamina  burner, one of the best when you have spared enough stamina (You will need Guild [Tip6]) for these.  

 **Tip9**
You can switch on Auto join so you will get some rewards by joining your own guild rallies [Guild tab > War > Enable Auto join], 1 of your Queues should be in town and it will auto join other rallies! (Works when you are offline). 

 **Tip10**
Once Yeti , Black Dragon or Troll appear on the world map, there is no need to rally the Boars anymore! Focus these 3 for they have a great dark medals shop. 

 **Tip11**
By now you should have joined an active guild! So keep up with their chat and if they relocate on the map, then make sure to follow them by answering the invitation to the guild assembly point (found at the top of your guild chat currently).

 **Tip12** 
If your guild owns a ruin! You can relocate anywhere inside it using guild relocation! [Guild tab > Store > pick for 300 coins], you can't shield when you relocate exactly on the ruin (sand area) it is a pvp square so if you want to shield locate your castle on the green fields next to it. 

 **TIP13**
Upgrade your :Barracks: Barracks! Do not forget about them, especially the barrack of your best hero/s. 

 **Tip14**
Attacking world mobs, rallying world bosses  can cause soldiers losses! If you can't afford to sustain them, hit a lower level of what you can defeat currently. 

 **Tip15**
Manage your resources, do not unpack the itemized      unless you are going to use them it is a war game after all, someone will come for it!

##  Lord's Hall 
* Both Traits are accessible in passive Trees, via The __Lord's Hall__ building that is available at Castle level 25. 

##  Glory Medals 
* Glory Medal is an essential currency used to upgrade The __Lord Hall__ providing __Lord Points__ with every level gained. 
* The only source to gain Glory Medals currently is through participating in __Guild Arms Race__ event. 

##  Lord's Points
* Lord's point Is the core item to __acquire and level up__ Lord's Traits, and as mentioned above it can be gained by upgrading The Lord Hall.

##  Lord's Traits selection 
* As we have two options here! One of the important things to know is what to pick so let's break it down quickly! Before we dive in the details.

##  Harvest Lord! Why & When?

* Harvest lord is the first option to pick for the majority of players for it provides them with __nice construction buff__, speeding up their Journy to Castle and Barracks level 40.

* Additionally it is a safe options for the Free to play community, or Peace lovers early game! As they can have tools to support their fighters by constructing __Dark&Light Towers__ for them on the map, without getting their hands dirty in a melee.

##   War Lord! Why & When & __ How to Reset__?


* Opposed to the Harvest Lord, War Lord is often chosen later on, mainly because it requires more Lord Points and is less useful for growth. It can be switched from Harvest Lord to War Lord by using the  Lord Trait Reset Scroll, which can be purchased in the Daily Diamond Shop. There is also a free weekly reset. This switch is recommended as soon as the player upgrades to Castle level 40 or whenever they need more combat power.

* War Lord however, by default is a great fit for players who are invested in PVP, and are __regularly spending__ to stay on top, Making this pick the best of both in this scenario Early, and End game, actually the ultimate choice for end game with no doubt.**

##  What is it and How does it work?
-
Server Transfer allows an individual player to move from Server X to Server Y according to a set of **rules**:

1. By default, Server Transfer will not be available for servers that are **younger** than 90 days.

2. The Transfer must occur within a specific Transfer period lasting 1 week. This period is referred to as a **Transfer round**.

3. Transferring your Castle from Server X to Y requires  Transfer Scrolls.

4. When the Transfer Round begins, you will have a **range** of Servers to choose from. You can:

* **Directly** transfer if your Castle power is **below** the threshold set by the target server’s king.

* If your power is **above** the threshold, the king of the target server must **manually** approve your request.

5. Your current server’s king cannot interfere with your transfer—only the king of your destination server can.

6. During the beta, server X could accept up to 50 players **MAX** from all eligible servers (e.g., T, U, V, W, Y, And Z) Together.

7. Transferring servers will place you on a **60-days cooldown**. 

##  When is the 1st Transfer round expected to happen?
-
* Round One is expected to take place in 10th to 16th of February.

* Round 1 will  include servers 1 to 144.

* This does not mean you can transfer between any of these servers, but these servers will have the transfer functionality enabled.

No.

 Build and use the 'Ancient Tree Branch' to upgrade the 'Spring of Nature';

Have guild members donate water for a temporary increase in water supply;
Occupy ruins, the environmental water supply in this area will greatly increase, everyone can enjoy this boost, regardless of whether the ruins are occupied by your guild.

Use it during sandstorms to help teammates avoid morale dropping to 0%.

 **Hero Attack:** The main source of power found in multiple sources such as gears, or through leveling heroes, without it no Damage can be dealt! It is a flat Value.  

 **Hero HP:** The main Life source of heroes, it is the main defensive stat, however some heroes utilize it by using a % of it's total to deal Damage through their skills, such as Rose or Tide caller. 

 **Hero Defense:** It is a flat value that is __countered__ by the enemy __Defense break__, the remaining value then gets converted into Damage reduction at a specific ratio.

So defense = % damage reduction. 
Defense break reduces that by reducing the defense itself.

 **Critical Rate:** a % chance to deal a __Critical strike__, amplifying the damage dealt through auto attacks and skills including ultimates when it triggers. 

 **Critical resistance:** A defensive stat that takes each Critical strike, and counter it individually for a chance to turn it into a normal strike again. 

 **Critical Damage:** The % of damage added to an auto attack or skill when a Critical strike successfully applied and passed through the critical resistance.

 **Dodge:** Defensive mechanism that grants % Chance to __completely Evade__ a hit or skill, taking 0 damage in that instance.

 **Hit:** An offensive mean to counter Dodge, if it is higher than Dodge on the enemies' heroes that means the Enemy's heroes won't have any chance to avoid your damage. 

 **Block:** Defensive mechanism that allows your hero to block a strike, When a strike is blocked, 50% of its damage is reduced. 

 **Block break:** An Offensive mean to counter Block, __works similar to Hit__. 

 **Damage:** Multiplier to the Attack attribute, and it can be found on multiple sources, it also has multiple forms, such as Normal hit damage or Skill damage, each applies where it should. 

 **Damage reduction:** Opposite to damage, but similar in concept! For example  Skill damage reduction reduce the damage taken from a skill hit by %. 

 **Hero Healing Rate:** Increases the healing given by a healer to it's target.

 **Hero Healing received:** Amplifies the healing heroes receives from all sources.

**Pets can be hatched from eggs, which you can find and incubate in the  Pet Paradise building. Here’s the step-by-step process:

__Collecting Eggs__
Eggs are obtainable through special bounties, with 3 free bounties available daily and 1 additional bounty for 1,000 gems.

Each bounty can spawn a Slime Boss (Pet Monster), You can attack it solo or with a rally (though both approaches yield the same results).

After defeating the Slime Boss, you have a chance to earn an egg and will always receive pet food.

Level 1 Pet Monster has a chance of 33.3% to drop an egg. 
Level 2 Pet Monster at (Pet Paradise Level 35) has slightly higher chance to drop an egg, and so on. 

__Capturing and Hatching Eggs__
To hatch an egg, first capture it by sending a Queue to it. 

Allies can assist in moving the egg to your castle.

Once in your castle, you can hatch the egg in Pet Paradise.

 Egg Rarity and Drop Chances
Eggs come in two colors, each with different odds of hatching a rare pet:

__Blue Egg__
20% chance to hatch a Blue (Rare) pet.
If unsuccessful, a Green (Common) Slime pet will hatch.

__Green Egg__
10% chance to hatch a Blue (Rare) pet.
Otherwise, it will hatch a Green (Common) Slime pet.**
